What is term life insurance?

Term life insurance provides protection for a specific period ranging from 5 to 30 years. If you pass away during the term, your beneficiaries receive a tax-free benefit. Giving you straightforward, budget-friendly coverage.

How does term life insurance differ from other types?

Term life focuses on pure protection. It does not build cash value and the policy ends when the term expires unless you renew it. Many policies include options to renew or to convert to permanent coverage within a set window, usually without new medical exams. Compared with permanent insurance, term life offers a lower initial cost for a larger amount of coverage.

Who is term life insurance best suited for?

Term life fits people who want strong protection during high-need years. It works well for covering essential costs. It is also a good choice if you want the most coverage for the lowest cost today.

When deciding if this kind of coverage is right for you, it helps to understand its core features. Here is a concise overview of what term life can provide, so you can feel informed and confident as you evaluate your options.

01 Fixed Payments

With no cash value component, this policy offers lower premiums compared to other life insurance options.

02 Flexible Coverage Options

You can choose from a range of high coverage amounts to match your unique situation and financial needs.

03 Tax-Free Payout to Beneficiaries

The death benefit, paid to your beneficiaries, is tax-free, offering financial support to those you leave behind, including family members and designated executors.

04 Conversion Flexibility

Your term policy can be converted to permanent insurance while it’s active, provided you’re under the age limit. At the end of the term, you can choose to renew, convert to permanent coverage without a medical exam, or let the policy lapse.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when the term expires?
When your term ends, you typically have three options: renew the coverage, convert it to a permanent life insurance policy, or allow it to end.
Who is term life insurance best suited for?
Term life insurance is a practical choice for individuals seeking simple, cost-effective protection, often to cover financial responsibilities such as income replacement, a mortgage, or raising children.
Is a medical exam required?
Some term policies require a medical exam, while others offer simplified or no-medical-exam options depending on the coverage amount and provider.
Can I convert my term policy to permanent insurance?
Yes. Most term policies include a conversion option, allowing you to transition to permanent insurance without the need for a new medical exam.
