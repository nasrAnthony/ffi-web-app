What is disability insurance?

Disability insurance ensures you can focus on getting better by replacing a portion of your income with a monthly benefit if you’re unable to work due to a covered illness or injury. Benefits help pay everyday bills, rent or mortgage, groceries, loans, and childcare.

How does it work?

Disability insurance provides income replacement if you are unable to work due to illness or injury. The benefits can last for varying lengths of time, depending on your policy, offering support for anything from a few months to several years, or even until retirement. Disability insurance offers monthly income replacement, typically covering 70–85% of your pre-disability earnings.

Who is it best suited for?

Anyone who relies on their income. This includes, employees, contractors, professionals, and business owners. It’s especially helpful if you have fixed obligations or limited emergency savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does coverage last?
Coverage duration varies by plan. Short-term plans may provide support for several months, while long-term options can extend until a specified age. Contact us to determine the best option based on your needs and risk exposure.
Are pre-existing conditions covered?
Coverage for pre-existing conditions depends on the policy. Some plans may have exclusions. It’s important to discuss your specific situation with us to ensure clarity on what is covered.
Is there a waiting period before benefits are paid?
Yes. Most policies include a waiting period, also known as an “elimination period,” which can range from a few weeks to several months, and in some cases up to two years.
Are disability benefits taxable?
If your employer pays the premiums, benefits received are generally taxable. If you pay the premiums yourself, the benefits are typically received tax-free.
