What is critical illness insurance?

Critical illness insurance pays a one-time lump-sum benefit if you’re diagnosed with a covered condition, such as cancer, heart attack, or stroke (as defined in the policy). You can use the funds however you need—treatment, travel, time off work, childcare, or everyday bills—so you can focus on recovery.

How does it work?

If a covered condition is diagnosed and the policy requirements are met (including any waiting and survival periods), the benefit is paid directly to you. Coverage is typically offered for set terms (e.g., to age 65/75/100) with level premiums, and many plans include options such as Return of Premium.

Who is it best suited for?

CI coverage is valuable for families and individuals who want a financial cushion during a serious health event, business owners who need to keep operations stable while they recover, and anyone whose budget would be strained by treatment costs or time away from work.

Key features of a
critical illness insurance policy

A serious diagnosis can disrupt life in many ways. Here are the core features that make critical illness coverage a practical safety net while you focus on getting well.

01 Lump-Sum Benefit

Cash you can use for what matters

Receive a one-time payment upon a covered diagnosis. Use it for treatment, income replacement, travel to specialists, home care, or everyday expenses—no receipts required.

02 Covered Conditions

Protection for major health events

Plans typically cover conditions such as life-threatening cancer, heart attack, and stroke, plus additional illnesses defined in the contract. Exact definitions and lists vary by insurer—your advisor will help compare.

03 Waiting & Survival Periods

Benefit timing that aligns with recovery

Policies include waiting/survival periods that must be met after diagnosis before benefits are payable. We’ll walk you through the timelines so there are no surprises at claim time.

04 Premiums & Terms

Level costs over a clear coverage period

Choose coverage to a target age (e.g., 65/75/100). Premiums are typically level for the chosen term, with options to adjust coverage as your needs change.

05 Optional Add-Ons

Customize for your family and goals

Many plans offer Return of Premium (on surrender or claim), partial payouts for early-stage conditions, or child coverage options. We’ll tailor a solution that fits your budget and priorities.

Frequently asked questions

How much coverage do I need?
A common approach is to estimate 6–24 months of after-tax income plus any expected treatment or travel costs. We’ll size coverage to your budget and priorities.
What conditions are covered?
Coverage varies by insurer and is defined precisely in the policy wording. Core conditions often include life-threatening cancer, heart attack, and stroke, with additional illnesses depending on the plan.
What affects my price?
Age, health, coverage amount, contract term (to age 65/75/100), and optional features like Return of Premium all impact the premium. We compare carriers to find strong value.
