What is critical illness insurance?

Critical illness insurance pays a one-time lump-sum benefit if you’re diagnosed with a covered condition, such as cancer or a heart attack. You can use the funds to help you cover travel, time off work, childcare, or everyday bills so you can focus on recovery.

How does it work?

If a covered condition is diagnosed and the policy requirements are met, the benefit is paid directly to you. Coverage is typically offered for set terms with level premiums, and many plans include options such as Return of Premium.

Who is it best suited for?

This coverage is valuable for families and individuals who want a financial cushion during a serious health event, business owners who need to keep operations stable while they recover, and anyone whose budget would be strained by treatment costs or time away from work.

Key features of a
critical illness insurance policy

A serious diagnosis can disrupt life in many ways. Here are the core features that make critical illness coverage a practical safety net while you focus on getting well.

01 Lump-Sum Benefit

Receive a one-time payment upon a covered diagnosis. Use it for treatment, income replacement, travel to specialists, home care, or everyday expenses.

02 Covered Conditions

Plans typically cover conditions such as life-threatening cancer, heart attack, and stroke, plus additional illnesses defined in the contract.

03 Flexibility

You have the flexibility to use the payout for any expenses related to your condition. This is not limited to just medical costs.

04 Affordable Protection

There is always an option for everyone. You can select protection plans that suit your current lifestyle and budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can benefit from critical illness insurance?
This coverage is ideal for individuals who want to protect their family or finances in the event of a serious health issue, or for those with a family history of critical illnesses.
Which conditions are generally covered?
Most plans provide protection for major conditions such as cancer, heart attacks, strokes, and organ failure. If you have specific health concerns, we can review your options in detail.
Can I obtain coverage with a pre-existing condition?
It depends on the policy. Some plans may have waiting periods, exclusions, or modified terms. Contact us to discuss your individual situation and available options.
How do I file a claim if diagnosed with a critical illness?
If diagnosed, notify us immediately. We’ll guide you through submitting the necessary medical documentation and assist with filing the claim with your insurer.
