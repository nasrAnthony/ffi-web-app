What is universal life insurance?

Universal life is permanent coverage with flexibility. You choose how much to fund within minimum and maximum limits, and you can adjust the death benefit over time. The policy builds tax-advantaged cash value that earns interest or is linked to market strategies, and this value can be accessed through loans or withdrawals when available.

How does universal life insurance differ from other types?

Compared with whole life, universal life offers more control over premiums and the death benefit. Growth potential depends on credited rates or market performance rather than fixed guarantees. It usually requires periodic reviews to ensure funding keeps the policy in force. Compared with term insurance, universal life lasts for life and builds cash value, although the initial cost is higher.

Who is universal life insurance best suited for?

Universal life suits people who want lifelong protection with flexibility. It works well for families and business owners who may want to change coverage, for clients seeking tax-efficient accumulation, and for those comfortable monitoring funding levels and performance with an advisor.

Universal Life Insurance serves as an effective tool for transferring wealth with tax advantages. By growing your savings within the policy, it supports estate planning strategies that reduce tax liabilities, allowing you to protect and transfer your assets more efficiently.

Frequently asked questions

How does the investment component work?
The policy offers a selection of investment opportunities that allow your earnings to grow without being taxed until you withdraw them.
Can I withdraw money from my policy?
Yes, you have the option to withdraw from the policy's cash value. Keep in mind that doing so could reduce the amount of your death benefit.
Is this policy beneficial for estate planning?
Absolutely, this policy can help reduce your tax burden and allows for a seamless transfer of assets to your heirs.
What happens if I stop paying premiums?
If you cease premium payments, the accumulated cash value can be used to cover the costs. However, long-term non-payment may decrease the death benefit.
