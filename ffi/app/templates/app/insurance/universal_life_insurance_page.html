What is universal life insurance?

Universal life is permanent coverage with flexibility. You choose how much to fund within minimum and maximum limits, and you can adjust the death benefit over time. The policy builds tax-advantaged cash value that earns interest or is linked to market strategies, and this value can be accessed through loans or withdrawals when available.

How does universal life insurance differ from other types?

Compared with whole life, universal life offers more control over premiums and the death benefit, and growth potential depends on credited rates or market performance rather than fixed guarantees. It usually requires periodic reviews to ensure funding keeps the policy in force. Compared with term insurance, universal life lasts for life and builds cash value, although the initial cost is higher.

Who is universal life insurance best suited for?

Universal life suits people who want lifelong protection with flexibility. It works well for families and business owners who may want to change coverage, for clients seeking tax-efficient accumulation, and for those comfortable monitoring funding levels and performance with an advisor.

Key features of a
universal life insurance policy

When deciding if this kind of coverage is right for you, it helps to understand its core features. Here is a concise overview of what term life can provide, so you can feel informed and confident as you evaluate your options.

01 Lifetime Protection

Permanent coverage with an adjustable death benefit

Universal life provides lifelong insurance. You can request changes to the death benefit as needs evolve, subject to product rules and underwriting where required.

02 Flexible Premiums

Control how and when you fund the policy

Premiums can be varied within minimum and maximum limits. You may pay more in strong years or reduce payments later, provided the policy has sufficient value to stay in force.

03 Cash Value Growth

Tax-advantaged value that can build over time

Cash value earns credited interest or follows chosen strategies, depending on the product. Growth is tax-advantaged while it remains inside the policy, helping support long-term goals.

04 Investment Choices

Choose fixed, indexed, or market-linked options

Depending on the policy, you can allocate cash value to fixed interest, index-linked strategies, or market-linked accounts. Regular reviews help keep funding and allocations aligned with your objectives and risk comfort.

05 Access & Loans

Access cash value when you need flexibility

Many policies allow withdrawals or policy loans. Outstanding loans and withdrawals reduce cash value and the death benefit and may have tax implications, so guidance from an advisor is important.
