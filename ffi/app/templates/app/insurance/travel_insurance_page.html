What is travel insurance?

Travel insurance helps protect your trip—and your wallet—when unexpected events happen. Core options include Emergency Medical coverage while you’re away, Trip Cancellation (before you depart), Trip Interruption (after you depart), and Baggage / Travel Delay benefits. It’s designed to cover costs your provincial plan or airline won’t.

How does it work?

You choose the plan(s) that fit your trip. If a covered event occurs—like a medical emergency abroad, severe weather that prevents departure, or lost baggage—you can access 24/7 assistance and submit a claim for eligible expenses (subject to limits, exclusions, and deductibles). For cancellation, buying soon after booking maximizes protection for pre-departure events.

Who is it best suited for?

Canadians travelling out of province or out of country, families on vacation, business travellers, students studying away, snowbirds needing longer stays, and visitors to Canada who need emergency medical protection. Plans can be tailored for single trips or multiple trips per year.

Key features of a
travel insurance policy

Trips don’t always go as planned. These features help you manage costs and get help quickly, so you can focus on the journey.

01 Emergency Medical

24/7 help for unexpected care abroad

Coverage for eligible hospital, physician, ambulance, and prescription costs while travelling. Includes access to a 24/7 assistance team and coordination of care; direct billing where available.

02 Trip Cancellation / Interruption

Protect non-refundable trip costs

Reimburses prepaid, non-refundable expenses if you must cancel or cut a trip short for a covered reason (e.g., sudden illness, severe weather, certain travel advisories). Terms vary by insurer.

03 Baggage & Delay

Support when plans (or bags) go missing

Benefits for lost, stolen, or delayed baggage, and for trip or flight delays—covering essentials like toiletries, clothing, meals, and accommodation up to policy limits.

04 Medical Stability

Pre-existing condition rules

Coverage for pre-existing conditions depends on stability periods and medical questionnaires. We’ll compare carriers and clarify how stability is defined for your age and health profile.

05 Plans & Add-Ons

Build a plan for how you travel

Single-trip or multi-trip annual, family plans, snowbird top-ups, visitors-to-Canada medical, rental car damage, and adventure-sports upgrades—customized to your itinerary and budget.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need travel medical if I have a provincial plan?
Yes—provincial health plans offer limited out-of-province/out-of-country coverage and typically don’t cover costly items like medical evacuation. Travel medical helps bridge the gap.
When should I buy trip cancellation coverage?
As soon as you book. Buying early maximizes protection for pre-departure events. Some plans have time-sensitive requirements—your advisor will confirm the deadlines for your itinerary.
Are pre-existing conditions covered?
It depends on the plan’s definition of stability and your medical history. Some policies cover stable conditions after a set period; others require a questionnaire. We’ll compare options for you.
What coverage limit should I choose?
Higher limits provide greater protection for emergencies and evacuations. We’ll recommend limits that align with your destination, trip length, and budget.
