What is whole life insurance?

Whole life insurance provides lifelong coverage, so your beneficiaries are supported whenever you pass away. Premiums stay level for the entire policy, which adds stability and predictability. The policy also builds cash value over time, giving you access to savings you can use later or borrow against if needed.

How does whole life insurance differ from other types?

Term life lasts for a set period, while whole life remains in force for your entire lifetime. Premiums are fixed, coverage is guaranteed for life, and cash value grows over time. Many participating policies may also pay dividends that can increase value or reduce out of pocket cost. These features are not typical of other life insurance options.

Who is whole life insurance best suited for?

Whole life fits people who want long term financial stability, estate planning benefits, and a policy that steadily builds value. It offers lasting protection for your family and can serve as a financial asset that grows with you.

Key features of a
whole life insurance policy

When deciding if this kind of coverage is right for you, it helps to understand its core features. Here is a concise overview of what whole life can provide, so you can feel informed and confident as you evaluate your options.

01 Death Benefit

A guaranteed payment for your beneficiaries

Your policy provides a tax-free benefit to your beneficiaries when you pass away. It is designed to protect loved ones, help settle debts, and support longer-term goals such as education or legacy giving.

02 Level Premiums

Predictable costs that do not change

Premiums are fixed for the life of the policy, which makes budgeting straightforward and protects you from future rate increases due to age or health changes.

03 Cash Value

Value that grows inside the policy

A portion of your premium builds cash value over time. This can be accessed through policy loans or withdrawals, offering flexibility for future needs while the coverage remains in place.

04 Dividends*

Potential to enhance long-term value

Participating policies may pay dividends that can purchase paid-up additions, reduce premiums, or be taken in cash. Dividends are not guaranteed and vary by carrier performance.

05 Access & Loans

Options when you need funds

You can borrow against the policy’s cash value or make withdrawals where available. Loans reduce available cash value and the death benefit until repaid, so we help you plan thoughtfully.

Frequently asked questions

How much coverage do I need?
Many families target 7–10× annual income plus debts and future goals. We’ll tailor it to your budget.
Does term convert to permanent?
Most term policies allow conversion to permanent coverage without medicals within a set window.
What affects my price?
Age, health, coverage amount, term length, and riders. We compare carriers to find value.
