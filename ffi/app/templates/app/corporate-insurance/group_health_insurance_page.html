What is group health insurance?

Group health insurance (employee benefits) provides coverage for your employees and their families— typically including extended health care, prescription drugs, dental, vision, paramedical services, emergency travel medical, and more. Plans can also bundle life, AD&D, and disability insurance.

How does it work for employers?

You choose a plan design, funding model, and eligibility rules (e.g., waiting periods, employee classes). The insurer (or administrator) handles claims and 24/7 support. Funding options include fully insured, ASO/self-funded with stop-loss, and Health Spending Accounts (HSA)—or a hybrid. We benchmark carriers, negotiate rates, and manage renewals and compliance.

Who is it best suited for?

Corporations of all sizes—from startups building their first benefits plan to established firms optimizing costs. Plans can be tailored by department or location using employee classes and contribution strategies.

Frequently asked questions

How many employees do we need to start a plan?
Plans can start with only a few employees, though certain benefits and underwriting rules vary by group size and industry. We’ll advise on minimums and the most cost-effective structure for your headcount.
Fully insured vs. ASO—what’s the difference?
Fully insured offers predictable premiums and insurer risk. ASO/self-funded reimburses actual claims with a monthly admin fee and stop-loss protection for large claims. Hybrids (e.g., HSA + insured) balance flexibility and certainty.
What drives renewal increases?
Claims experience, demographics, trend/inflation (especially drugs and dental), pooling charges, and plan changes. We use market benchmarking and design levers to manage renewals proactively.
Are benefits taxable?
Tax treatment can vary by benefit type and province (e.g., Québec differs). We’ll coordinate with your payroll and tax advisors to implement the appropriate setup for employer/employee contributions.
