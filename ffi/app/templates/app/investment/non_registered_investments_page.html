What is a non-registered account?

A standard taxable investment account with no contribution limits or withdrawal restrictions. You can hold ETFs, mutual funds, stocks, bonds, and GICs. Perfect for overflow savings or goals that need flexibility.

Why it’s useful

Unlimited contributions, joint ownership options, and the ability to realize capital gains on your own schedule. It also complements registered accounts for asset-location and tax planning.

Good fit

Savers who’ve maxed registered room, business owners with variable income, or anyone wanting access to funds without registered-plan withdrawal rules.

How are returns taxed?

In a non-registered account, earnings are taxable annually depending on type:

  • Interest — fully taxable in the year earned.
  • Eligible dividends — preferential dividend tax treatment.
  • Capital gains — only the taxable portion is included when realized.

How we optimize

We use tax-aware asset location, deferred rebalancing windows, and—when appropriate—tax-loss harvesting to manage realized gains and keep portfolios efficient.

What do we invest in?

We build diversified, low-cost portfolios using broad-market ETFs or curated funds, plus cash & GIC ladders for near-term needs. Automatic contributions and periodic rebalancing keep things on track.

Risk & time horizon

Your mix of equities, bonds, and cash is matched to your timeline and comfort with volatility. Short-term goals skew safer; long-term growth leans more toward equities.

When should I use non-registered?

A common sequence is: emergency fund → FHSA (if eligible) → TFSA/RRSP mix → RESP for kids → then non-registered for overflow savings or flexible goals. We’ll tailor this to your cash-flow and tax bracket.

Who benefits most?

High savers who’ve maxed registered room, investors needing access before retirement, and business owners who coordinate with corporate/cash management.

Frequently asked questions

Non-registered vs. registered—how do I choose?
Use registered accounts first for tax advantages when available. Then use non-registered for flexibility and overflow savings. We’ll map the right mix for your situation.
What can I hold?
Diversified ETFs/funds, stocks, bonds, and GICs. We’ll match the mix to your timeline and rebalance periodically.
How are taxes reported?
Your institution issues annual slips for interest, dividends, and realized capital gains/losses. We coordinate with your tax professional as needed.
Can I minimize taxes?
Yes—through asset location, timing of gains, and (when appropriate) tax-loss harvesting. We design with tax in mind, without letting tax alone drive investment risk.
