Key Benefits of
Non-Registered Investing

Non-registered accounts add flexibility once registered room is used and let you fine-tune taxes, liquidity, and risk across all your accounts.

01Maximized Growth

You can invest without limits, allowing your wealth to grow freely. This is particularly valuable for those who have maxed out contributions in other accounts, like RRSPs and TFSAs.

02Tax Efficiency

Non-registered investments offer tax advantages, as capital gains are taxed less than regular income. By employing strategic tax planning, you can reduce your tax burden and enhance your investment growth over time.

03Flexibility

Non-registered investments provide the freedom to access your funds whenever needed, without the penalties or limitations typically found in registered accounts. This flexibility is especially beneficial for those with fluctuating financial requirements.

Corporate insured retirement plans

Corporate Insured Retirement Plans (CIRPs) are a tax-efficient tool for building retirement savings within a company. They combine insurance with investment growth, offering a stable financial strategy for business owners and key employees.

Corporate owned life insurance

Corporate Owned Life Insurance (COLI) offers business owners a way to grow wealth tax-deferred within their company. It serves as both an investment tool to provide liquidity and a means to enhance estate planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are withdrawals taxed?
Yes, withdrawals are taxed on earnings, such as capital gains and dividends, though capital gains are taxed at a lower rate.
How does this differ from an RRSP?
Non-registered investments have no contribution limits and allow penalty-free withdrawals at any time, offering greater flexibility.
Can I invest in different asset types?
Yes, you can diversify your investments in non-registered accounts, including options like stocks, bonds, and mutual funds.
Who is this suitable for?
These accounts are ideal for those who’ve maxed out their RRSP/TFSA contributions and need flexible access to funds before retirement.
