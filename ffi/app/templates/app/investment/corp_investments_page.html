Tax-efficient growth strategies to minimize liabilities

Our corporate portfolios are structured to maximize after-tax return on business assets. We consider interest vs. eligible dividends, capital gains realization, and the balance between passive income and operating requirements—so growth compounds while liabilities stay controlled.

  • Asset location & income-type tilts (interest / dividends / capital gains).
  • Timing of dispositions & loss-harvesting windows when appropriate.
  • Low-cost, diversified building blocks; clear reporting for your accountant.

Investment accounts tailored for business needs

We map the right account types to your objectives—reinvesting retained earnings, funding employee programs, or structuring assets for future acquisitions.

  • Corporate cash & investment accounts (operating surplus, reserve funds).
  • Trust & holding company structures, coordinated with your tax team.
  • Employee savings/benefit plans (e.g., Group RRSP, DPSP) with clear governance.

Dividend reinvestment & structured asset allocation

We implement disciplined dividend reinvestment and a rules-based allocation across equities, bonds, cash, and alternatives. The result: compounding over time and risk aligned with both current operations and long-term plans.

  • DRIP and cash-sweep policies to keep idle cash working.
  • Target bands with periodic rebalancing; stress-tested for drawdowns.
  • Separate “Growth” vs “Reserve” sleeves to match objectives.

Liquidity management for expansion & stability

We set explicit liquidity targets for operating needs, upcoming projects, and contingencies—so you’re ready for opportunities without compromising long-term profitability.

  • Tiered cash & GIC ladders for predictable spends.
  • Scenario planning for acquisitions and cap-ex.
  • Integrated cash-flow forecasts and investment policy statements (IPS).

Frequently asked questions

How do you coordinate with my accountant?
We align IPS and reporting with your tax strategy, share year-end summaries, and plan realized gains/losses around cash-flow needs.
Can we separate operating cash from long-term reserves?
Yes—distinct sleeves and accounts (“Operating”, “Reserve”, “Growth”) with different risk and liquidity targets.
What do you invest in?
Diversified ETFs and high-quality funds, bonds, and cash/GIC ladders—kept low-cost and tax aware.
How quickly can funds be accessed?
Operating and short-term reserves are laddered for near-term dates; growth sleeves can be raised to cash per your IPS and timelines.
