Frequently asked questions

Which account should I use first?
It depends on your tax bracket and goal timing. Common order: emergency fund (HISA) → FHSA (if eligible) → RRSP/TFSA mix → RESP for kids → non-registered once registered room is used.
How are investments taxed in Canada?
RRSPs are tax-deferred; withdrawals are taxable. TFSAs are tax-free. Non-registered accounts incur tax on interest, eligible dividends, and capital gains. We structure portfolios to reduce tax drag where possible.
Are my assets safe?
We use custodians that provide account safeguards (e.g., CIPF/CDIC eligibility where applicable). You retain account ownership; we provide advice and management.
What are your fees?
Transparent advisory pricing with no lock-ins. We favor low-cost ETFs and institutional funds to keep total costs competitive. We’ll show a clear breakdown before you commit.
