Why Group RRSPs?

A Group RRSP offers a simple and efficient retirement savings plan, benefiting both employees and employers by providing a clear path to secure retirement savings.

How It Supports Your Business

Employees can make contributions directly through payroll deductions, gaining tax benefits while building their savings. Employers may also choose to match these contributions, providing additional tax advantages for the business.

Who Can Benefit

Employees will be given the convenience of automatic savings with the added perk of employer-matched contributions. For employers, this plan helps create an attractive benefits package, which is key to recruiting and retaining skilled talent.

Key features of a
Group RRSP

A well-designed Group RRSP creates a win-win: employees save automatically with tax advantages, and employers deliver a competitive benefit with clear, controllable costs.

01 Employee Satisfaction

Offering a Group RRSP as part of a comprehensive benefits package is a great way to attract and retain top talent. Employees value the chance to save for retirement, especially when there’s the added incentive of employer-matching contributions.

02 Immediate Tax Savings

Employees can benefit from tax savings right away through payroll deductions. Contributions are made before taxes, which reduces taxable income and provides an immediate benefit when filing taxes. This makes saving for retirement more accessible and attractive.

03 Cost-Effective for Employers

Employer contributions to a Group RRSP are tax-deductible, helping businesses reduce their overall tax burden. This enables employers to provide a valuable benefit to employees without a significant financial strain, making it a wise financial strategy for growing companies.

04 Retirement Readiness

A Group RRSP simplifies retirement savings for employees by using automatic payroll deductions. This ensures consistent contributions to retirement funds, promoting financial security in the future. With contributions from employers, employees are better prepared for a comfortable retirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Group RRSP compare to a pension plan?
Unlike pension plans, which often require mandatory contributions, Group RRSPs offer employees more flexibility, allowing them to contribute at their own discretion based on their financial needs.
Can employees access their funds before retirement?
Yes, employees can withdraw funds early, though these withdrawals are subject to tax. Programs like the Home Buyers’ Plan and Lifelong Learning Plan allow for tax-free withdrawals under certain conditions.
Are employer contributions required?
Employer contributions are optional. However, many employers choose to match employee contributions to encourage participation and accelerate retirement savings.
What happens if an employee leaves the company?
When an employee leaves, they can transfer their RRSP funds to another registered plan without facing penalties, ensuring they keep control of their retirement savings even after changing jobs.
