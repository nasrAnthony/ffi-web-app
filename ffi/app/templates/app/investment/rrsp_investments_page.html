What is a Group RRSP?

A Group RRSP is a workplace retirement savings plan. Employees contribute through payroll deductions (which count toward their personal RRSP limits) and investments grow tax-deferred. Employers can add a matching contribution that’s tax-deductible to the business.

How it helps your company

A clear retirement program improves hiring and retention, simplifies saving for your team, and lets you offer a competitive total-rewards package with transparent costs and administration.

Who it’s right for

Employers who want a simple, high-impact benefit; growing teams that value automatic savings and optional employer matching; organizations replacing or complementing an existing plan.

Frequently asked questions

How do payroll contributions work?
Employees choose a rate; payroll deducts each period and remits to their Group RRSP account automatically.
Are employer matches deductible?
Yes—employer contributions are generally a deductible business expense and follow the employee’s RRSP limits.
Do employer dollars vest?
Group RRSP money belongs to the employee right away. If you want vesting, pair the plan with a DPSP for the employer portion.
Can employees withdraw while employed?
RRSP withdrawals are taxable and may be restricted by plan rules to encourage long-term saving. Exceptions include HBP/LLP under CRA rules.
What happens when someone leaves?
They typically transfer their balance to an individual RRSP or another eligible plan and continue investing independently.
What do employees invest in?
A curated, diversified lineup (index/balanced/target-date). We set sensible defaults and provide education.
How much admin is required?
We handle setup, onboarding, and provider coordination. Payroll sends deductions; the recordkeeper issues statements and tax slips.
